1132.WTF
========

Do these three things. Stop when you are in the meeting.

STEP 1 - Open the .apk on your phone. Allow install.

STEP 2 - Open 1132.WTF

LAUNCH - Follow the three buttons on the screen, in order.


On the screen
-------------

STEP 1 - Clear Zoom
         Tap the button. Then:
         Force stop → Storage → Clear storage
         Open Zoom. Join as a guest. Do not sign in.

STEP 2 - New phone user  (only if STEP 1 still fails)
         Tap the button. Add a user. Switch to it.
         Install Zoom there. Join as a guest.

STEP 3 - Join in browser
         Type the meeting number.
         Tap LAUNCH.
         Do not sign in as the old account.
