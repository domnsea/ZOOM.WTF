1132.WTF
========

Do these three things. Stop when you are in the meeting.

STEP 1 - On a Mac, open 1132WTF.xcodeproj in Xcode

STEP 2 - Plug in your iPhone. Press Run.

LAUNCH - On the phone, follow the three cards, in order.


On the screen
-------------

STEP 1 - Clear Zoom
         Tap Open Settings. Then:
         General → iPhone Storage → Zoom → Delete App
         Reinstall Zoom. Join as a guest. Do not sign in.

STEP 2 - Not possible on iPhone
         Skip this. iPhone has no second user.

STEP 3 - Join in browser
         Type the meeting number.
         Tap LAUNCH.
         Do not sign in as the old account.
