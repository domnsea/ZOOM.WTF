1132.WTF
========

Do these three things. Stop when you are in the meeting.

STEP 1 - Unzip this folder. Double-click 1132.WTF.vbs

STEP 2 - Click STEP 1 - Fix Zoom

LAUNCH - Zoom opens empty. Join.
         Do not sign in as the old account.


If Windows blocks the file
--------------------------

STEP 1 - Right-click the zip → Properties → tick Unblock → OK

STEP 2 - Unzip again. Double-click 1132.WTF.vbs

LAUNCH - Click STEP 1 - Fix Zoom


If STEP 1 still fails
---------------------

STEP 1 - Click STEP 2 - Deep fix. Click Yes. Allow admin.

STEP 2 - Wait. Zoom opens as a new Windows user.

LAUNCH - Join. Do not sign in as the old account.


If that still fails
-------------------

STEP 1 - Click STEP 3 - Join in browser

STEP 2 - Paste the meeting link (or leave blank)

LAUNCH - Join from the browser. Do not sign in as the old account.
