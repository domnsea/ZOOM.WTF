1132.WTF
========

Do these three things. Stop when you are in the meeting.

STEP 1 - Run:  ./install.sh

STEP 2 - Open 1132.WTF from your app launcher
         (or run:  1132wtf-gui)

LAUNCH - Click STEP 1 - Fix Zoom
         Zoom opens empty. Join.
         Do not sign in as the old account.


If STEP 1 still fails
---------------------

STEP 1 - Click STEP 2 - Deep fix. Type your password.

STEP 2 - Wait. Zoom opens as a new user.

LAUNCH - Join. Do not sign in as the old account.


If that still fails
-------------------

STEP 1 - Click STEP 3 - Join in browser

STEP 2 - Paste the meeting link (or leave blank)

LAUNCH - Join from the browser. Do not sign in as the old account.
