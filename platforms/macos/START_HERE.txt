1132 FRESH 1.0.35 — Mac only
============================

The zip file must be named 1132-FRESH-1.0.35-macos.zip.
If Downloads has 1132-FRESH-macos.zip or 1.0.34, that is the old file. Trash it.

Type your Mac password in Terminal.
Zoom opens on THIS desktop with no saved device id.
Quit Zoom and your regular Zoom is put back.

https://github.com/domnsea/ZOOM.WTF/raw/cursor/build-1132-wtf-all-platforms-c565/dist/1132-FRESH-1.0.35-macos.zip

STEP 2 - Join only in the window it opened.
LAUNCH - Quit Zoom when done.
