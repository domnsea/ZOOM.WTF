1132.WTF — macOS will not open the app
======================================

macOS is blocking the download. That is normal. Do this:

STEP 1 - Right-click INSTALL_TO_APPLICATIONS.command
         Click Open. Click Open again.

STEP 2 - If it still blocks:
         Click Done (not Move to Trash).
         System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

LAUNCH - Open 1132.WTF from Applications.
         Then follow START_HERE.txt


If that still fails, open Terminal, paste this, press Return:

xattr -cr ~/Downloads ~/Desktop /Applications/1132.WTF.app 2>/dev/null
find ~/Downloads ~/Desktop /Applications -name '1132.WTF.app' -type d -maxdepth 6 \
  -exec xattr -cr {} \; \
  -exec codesign --force --deep --sign - {} \; 2>/dev/null
open /Applications/1132.WTF.app 2>/dev/null \
  || open ~/Downloads/1132-FRESH-1.0.33-macos/1132.WTF.app 2>/dev/null \
  || open ~/Downloads/1132-FRESH-macos/1132.WTF.app 2>/dev/null \
  || open ~/Downloads/1132.WTF-macos-1.0.0/1132.WTF.app 2>/dev/null \
  || open ~/Downloads/1132.WTF-macos/1132.WTF.app 2>/dev/null
