1132.WTF — read this if macOS will not open the app
====================================================

macOS is saying the author cannot be verified. That is Gatekeeper. This app
is not signed with a paid Apple Developer certificate ($99/year), so macOS
treats it like any other file that arrived from the internet. It is not a
warning that the app is damaged.

Do ONE of these, in this order.

1. Right-click  (works on most Macs)
   Right-click INSTALL_TO_APPLICATIONS.command → Open → Open.
   If the app itself is what was blocked, right-click 1132.WTF.app → Open.

2. Privacy & Security  (macOS 13, 14, 15 Sequoia)
   After the dialog appears, click Done (not Move to Trash).
   Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
   Scroll to the bottom. Click Open Anyway. Confirm.

3. Terminal  (always works)
   Open Terminal (Spotlight, type Terminal, press Return).
   Paste this entire block, then press Return:

xattr -cr ~/Downloads ~/Desktop /Applications/1132.WTF.app 2>/dev/null
find ~/Downloads ~/Desktop /Applications -name '1132.WTF.app' -type d -maxdepth 6 \
  -exec xattr -cr {} \; \
  -exec codesign --force --deep --sign - {} \; 2>/dev/null
open /Applications/1132.WTF.app 2>/dev/null \
  || open ~/Downloads/1132.WTF-macos-1.0.0/1132.WTF.app 2>/dev/null \
  || open ~/Downloads/1132.WTF-macos/1132.WTF.app 2>/dev/null

   Then double-click 1132.WTF in Applications, or the one in this folder.

That command only removes the "downloaded from the internet" flag from
1132.WTF and signs it as your own copy. It does not turn Gatekeeper off.
